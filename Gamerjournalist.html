Introducing: Moviedle. It may not be the most cleverly named Wordle variant, but it’s definitely one of the most clever executions. In this offshoot of the word game fever, you’ll be shown a sped-up, one-second-long version of a film. It’s then up to you to guess the name of said film. If you can’t guess the title, you can either take the L and guess anyway or click SKIP to slow the film down. But be careful! As with other Wordle variants, you only have six chances to get the correct answer!
