A new report has given Dino Crisis fans hope, which states that a Dino Crisis remake has been in development at Capcom since 2022.

Posted by Team Biohazard on Facebook on June 28, it’s claimed that the remake is “100000 real,” and could be announced in 2028.

“I’m saying this again for those who missed the info or still ask me the question sometimes. Dino Crisis Remake is 100000% real. Development began in 2022 and its announcement could be closer to 2028/2029 if we keep the schedule for a in Capcom remake.”

This is my first time hearing of Team Biohazard, so I’m not sure whether they’ve shared accurate information in the past. So as with all rumours, take it with a grain of salt until an official announcement.

Last year, Capcom released enhanced versions of Dino Crisis and Dino Crisis 2 on PC. Maybe this was Capcom testing the waters.
