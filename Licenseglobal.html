Jones Soda and Crayola Reunite on F&B Product

Jones Soda works with Crayola again to produce new limited-edition beverage product for the back-to-school season.

Jones Soda Co., a craft soda brand, has announced its collaboration with Crayola through a limited-edition six-pack launching during the back-to-school summer season. The program is part of Jones Soda's 30th anniversary, also known as the "Dirty 30" celebration, and reflects Jones Soda's strategy of building momentum through selective partnerships with brands that authentically align with its identity, including creativity, color, fun, nostalgia and community connection. Building on last year's Crayola collaboration, this year's six-pack features a refreshed design and a color palette that more closely reflects Jones Soda's look, while continuing to leverage Crayola's strong consumer recognition and multigenerational appeal. The limited-edition six-pack will be available at retail and online through Jones Soda's direct-to-consumer platform. Retail boxes will include six Crayola x Jones Soda bottles and one collectible Crayola x Jones Soda six-pack box. Direct-to-consumer orders will include one Crayola x Jones Soda six-pack box, four coloring pages and one eight-pack of crayons. "As we celebrate 30 years of Jones Soda, this collaboration represents the type of authentic partnership we believe can create meaningful brand momentum," says Scott Harvey, chief executive officer, Jones Soda Co. "Crayola is colorful, creative, nostalgic and deeply connected to community, making it a natural fit for Jones and our anniversary-year strategy."

The Crayola x Jones Soda six-pack will include six flavors, each inspired by a Crayola color:

  • Fruit Punch Soda, a brand-new Jones Soda flavor, inspired by Razzmatazz
  • Orange and Cream Soda inspired by Sunset Orange
  • Pineapple Cream Soda inspired by Dandelion
  • Green Apple Soda inspired by Granny Smith Apple
  • Berry Lemonade Soda inspired by Robin's Egg Blue
  • Fufu Berry Soda inspired by Razzle Dazzle Rose

The launch is designed to support retail visibility and online consumer demand during a high-traffic seasonal shopping window. By aligning with the back-to-school summer season, Jones Soda aims to tap into consumer nostalgia, family occasions, gifting and seasonal discovery while reinforcing its position as a premium craft soda brand with strong storytelling and collectible appeal. "At Crayola, we seek out partners who share our passion for inspiring creativity across generations, and this collaboration with Jones Soda does exactly that – just with a flavorful twist," says Anna Roca, head, global partnerships, Crayola. "It's a vibrant, multi-sensory experience that encourages fans of all ages to explore, create and rediscover the joy of color in a fresh, unexpected way."
